Prestigious Bakes

An online menu, order basket, order tracking, and admin dashboard for a bakery that was taking orders by chat.

Open the live site

The problem

The bakery sold cakes and small chops, and orders came in through chat messages. That made it easy to lose track of who ordered what, and customers had no way to check on their order.

What was built

  • An online menu with an order basket
  • Order tracking, so customers can follow their order
  • An admin dashboard where the owner manages every order
  • Mobile-first design that can be installed on a phone
